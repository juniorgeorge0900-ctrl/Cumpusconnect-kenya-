Privacy and Disclaimer

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Your information

This website does not ask you to create an account and does not collect personal details. The search box and filters work inside your own browser, and what you type is not sent to us.

If you email, call or message us on WhatsApp, we receive the details you choose to send, such as your name and number. We use them only to reply to you or check a listing.

Links to other websites

Each listing links to an official or third-party website. Those sites have their own privacy rules, and we are not responsible for them.

Disclaimer

Listings are shared for information only. Deadlines and requirements can change, so confirm everything on the official source before you apply. We do not guarantee that any application will succeed, and we are not responsible for decisions made by employers, universities or funders.

Protect yourself from scams

Never pay money to apply for a scholarship, job or internship. Do not share your ID number, bank details or passwords with anyone offering an opportunity. If something looks suspicious, tell us.

Changes

We may update this page from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
